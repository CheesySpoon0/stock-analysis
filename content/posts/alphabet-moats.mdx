## Durable advantages

Alphabet's blend of **distribution**, **datasets**, and **custom silicon** gives it optionality that few peers can match. YouTube engagement and Google Workspace stickiness create data loops that power model quality.

## Capital allocation

Massive free cash flow allows Alphabet to invest in AI infrastructure while still buying back shares. Management has shown discipline on operating expenses, letting incremental cloud margins shine through.

## Risk check

We monitor traffic acquisition costs, regulatory pressure, and the pace of AI-native search experiences. A spike in TAC without matching engagement would be a yellow flag.
