## Product velocity

Rapid category launches in dermatology, weight, and mental health are increasing lifetime value. The brand is resonating with cash-pay millennials who prize convenience and discretion.

## Retention trend

Cohort curves continue to flatten as the product mix widens. That makes marketing payback periods shorter and improves cash efficiency.

## What to watch

Execution risk around clinical operations and regulatory clarity for compounded drugs. We also monitor whether customer acquisition costs stay disciplined as the company scales paid media.
