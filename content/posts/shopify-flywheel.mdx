## Why now

Shopify's core subscription business is stable, but the torque now comes from **payments** and **logistics**. With Shop Pay adoption crossing 60% of GMV among eligible merchants, take rate expansion is showing up even in a muted macro tape.

## Signals that matter

- Attaching more products per merchant lowers churn.
- Fulfillment and Shop Pay increase conversion and checkout speed.
- The capital product gives Shopify data edge on merchant quality.

## What would change our mind

A decisive slowdown in attach rate for payments or the fulfillment network would break the operating leverage story. We also watch app ecosystem health closely—losing third-party innovation would make Shopify feel like a commodity cart again.
